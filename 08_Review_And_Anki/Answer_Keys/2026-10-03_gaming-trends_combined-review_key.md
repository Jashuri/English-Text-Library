## 4. Model Answers

These answers are examples. You can write shorter or longer answers, but they should be based on the texts.

1. How does the metaverse create a virtual, 3-dimensional game environment that allows players to interact with one another?

The metaverse creates a virtual, 3-dimensional game environment where players can chat, socialize, and interact with one another without being physically in the same place. It allows gamers to connect with a community they can resonate with while playing a shared game.

2. What are the key features of the metaverse that blur the lines between real-life conversations, social media, and gaming?

The key features of the metaverse are interconnected and interactive experiences. It imitates reality in its functions, but also incorporates fantastical elements, which gives games an intrigue factor and blurs the lines between real-life conversations, social media, and gaming.

3. How are realistic graphics expected to transform the gaming experience?

Realistic graphics are expected to transform gaming into a richer experience by making game environments more immersive and believable. Photorealistic graphics replicate real-life features similar to photographs, allowing gamers to relate more strongly to the game environment and its characters.

4. What is the role of ray tracing in creating photorealistic game environments?

Ray tracing plays an important role because it traces the path of light, including reflection, refraction, and shadows. Unlike traditional rendering, it follows a more precise approach and recreates subjects with photographic accuracy.

5. How do NFTs enhance the gaming experience and create opportunities for virtual ownership and monetisation?

NFTs enhance the gaming experience by allowing players to own, buy, sell, and trade digital assets. They create opportunities for virtual ownership and monetisation because gamers can purchase in-game assets, access exclusive digital spaces, and use NFTs inside and outside the game.

6. What are the unique characteristics of NFTs that make them valuable within and outside the game?

NFTs are valuable because they are unique, authentic, and cannot be replicated or interchanged. They can represent artwork, digital collectibles, weapons, virtual property, vehicles, or characters, and they can also be used on another NFT-based gaming platform.

7. How are the emerging trends of metaverse, realistic graphics, and NFT gaming expected to shape the future of the gaming industry?

These emerging trends are expected to make the gaming industry more immersive, interactive, and valuable for players. The metaverse can diversify and expand social gaming, realistic graphics can create authentic virtual game scenes, and NFT gaming can give players a stronger sense of actual ownership.
