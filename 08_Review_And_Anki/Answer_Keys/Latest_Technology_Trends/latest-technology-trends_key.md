## 3.4 Questions - Model Answers

1. What is AI well-known for?

AI is well-known for its presence in image and speech recognition, ride-sharing apps, mobile personal assistants, navigation apps, and various other applications.

2. What is the difference between 3G and 5G internet connection?

3G enabled online access and data-driven services on mobile devices, while 5G expands what is possible by using cutting-edge technology, including augmented reality and virtual reality. 5G also provides faster, high-speed access and allows complex applications to work almost anywhere.

3. What is edge computing used for?

Edge computing is used for low-latency and high-speed data processing. It allows computations to be carried out closer to data storage systems and can handle time-sensitive data stored in remote areas with minimal access to the central location.

4. Is it possible to follow customer's behaviour by means of the IoT?

Yes, it is possible. The Internet of Things collects data and insights that can be used in Internet of Behavior systems, so businesses can follow customer behaviour and use this information to benefit their channels.

5. What are the areas of our life where quantum computing can be used in?

Quantum computing can be used in banking and finance to monitor credit risk, perform high-frequency trading, and detect fraud. It can also be used in health-related areas, such as preventing the spread of coronavirus and developing potential vaccines.

6. What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a digital currency such as Bitcoin that is part of blockchain technology. Bitcoin is described as a decentralized digital ledger that keeps track of transactions through a global network of computers.

7. Why will cybersecurity remain a popular technology in the future?

Cybersecurity will remain popular because new threats constantly emerge and hackers continue trying to gain unauthorized access to data. As long as hackers exist, cybersecurity will be needed to improve defense.
